## Download

| System | File | How to install |
|---|---|---|
| macOS 11 or later (Apple silicon and Intel) | `TexCraft-{{VERSION}}-macos.dmg` | Open it and drag TexCraft onto Applications |
| Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) | `TexCraft-{{VERSION}}-windows-x64-setup.exe` | Run it (no administrator rights needed) |

Once installed, TexCraft updates itself. The `.zip` files and `latest.json` are what its updater
downloads; you do not need them.

**First launch.** These builds are not yet signed with an Apple or Microsoft certificate, so the
system asks once:

- **macOS 15 or later:** open TexCraft; when macOS says it cannot verify the app, go to System
  Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down and click **Open Anyway**. On macOS 14 or earlier,
  Control-click TexCraft in Applications and choose **Open**.
- **Windows:** if SmartScreen says "Windows protected your PC", click **More info** ›
  **Run anyway**.

TexCraft typesets with the TeX distribution installed on your computer, for example
[MacTeX](https://tug.org/mactex/), [MiKTeX](https://miktex.org/) or
[TeX Live](https://tug.org/texlive/).
